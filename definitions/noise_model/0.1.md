# Noise-model definitions 0.1

Status: complete working draft for prototyping and collaborator review. Version
1 is reserved for the first reviewed public release.

This document defines the human meaning of
`schemas/noise_model/0.1.schema.json`. A noise-model record is a compact,
citable registry entry. It deliberately does not attempt to encode a complete
mathematical or executable noise specification in database fields.

## Scope of the entry

`short_description` identifies the noise construction and states the scientific
scope intended by this registry entry. The exact realised probabilities used
for an experiment do not live here. They remain frozen in each referenced
circuit and detector-error-model files.

Two records which share a familiar family name are not interchangeable merely
because their names look similar. Results identify a circuit revision, and the
circuit identifies this exact noise-model entry.

## Primary specification

`paper_url` is the primary paper or permanent specification from which a reader
can recover the intended construction. It is part of the scientific provenance
of the entry, not a promise that the site has independently reproduced the
paper. Additional classified links may point to source, documentation, or
files.

## Randomisation of priors

`randomises_priors = true` means that applying the construction includes a
random choice which can change the exact numerical probabilities or weights
presented to the decoder between otherwise comparable circuit instances.
`false` means that randomisation of exact priors is not part of the construction
as registered.

This field describes the noise construction. A circuit does not copy it. The
actual priors for a particular circuit revision are the ones frozen in that
circuit and its detector error model.

## Credits and replacement

Credits are ordered and non-empty. Each credit is either a display-name string
or a registered account. Equal display-name strings on separate records do not
assert a shared person identity. The uploader is recorded separately.

Changing the scientific meaning of the description or
`randomises_priors` creates a new entry. The new entry may identify the old one
through `supersedes_noise_model_id`; supersession does not delete or rewrite the
old entry.

## Governance

`community` means a contributed entry, `official` means an admin-curated entry,
and `deprecated` means it should not be selected for new circuits. Promotion to
official is a governance action, not a change to the noise model and not a
scientific endorsement of results using it.

The lifecycle state, submitter, IDs, slugs, timestamps, and curation status are
identity, infrastructure, or governance fields. They do not define the noise
construction. There is deliberately no license field in this contract.
